Privacy policy

Fitness Data Bridge is maintained by Puh00. This policy describes the command-line tool and this informational website.

Data the tool accesses

When you configure fitness providers, the tool can retrieve the activity, workout, or wellness information requested by your command. Google integration reads and updates a training spreadsheet containing activity summaries and context you enter, such as goals, race plans, and lessons.

Google authorization uses the drive.file permission. This permits access to files created by the application or explicitly authorized for it. The tool does not request unrestricted access to all files in your Drive.

Purpose and storage

Data is used to retrieve training history, maintain your notebook, and support the training tasks you request. Google files remain in your Google account. Local OAuth refresh credentials are stored outside the source repository. If you configure a cloud environment, you supply a separate credential copy to that environment.

Access tokens are held in memory. Exported data remains wherever you choose to save it. Activity sync stores summaries, not a complete archive of original files or sensor streams.

Sharing and AI use

The tool sends requests to configured providers and writes requested records to your Google storage. It does not send training data to the project maintainer. When an AI agent runs the CLI, returned data can enter that agent's session and is handled under the AI provider's and your account's applicable settings and terms. Use narrow queries and file exports to limit session output.

The maintainer does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Fitness Data Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your controls

You control the notebook, exports, and credentials. Records remain until you remove them; sync does not automatically delete activities missing from a provider response. Revoke the application's access in your Google Account to prevent future authorized API access. Revocation does not delete existing files or cloud credential copies. Delete those separately from your Drive, device, or cloud environment when no longer needed.

This website

This static website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, login forms, or tracking cookies implemented by the application. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. External links follow their destination's privacy policies.

Contact and changes

For project questions, contact Puh00 on GitHub. Do not post credentials or private training data in public discussions. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy before they are introduced.
