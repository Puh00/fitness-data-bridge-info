Fitness Data Bridge is a command-line tool that reads fitness data, saves activity summaries to your Google Sheet, and keeps goals and plans available between sessions.

Fitness APIs → your private notebook → informed planning

How it works

Read activities from Intervals.icu and Garmin using your own credentials. Sync summaries to a private Google spreadsheet. Maintain goals, races, plans, and lessons there without storing personal training records in source control.

Planned cycling workouts can be uploaded to Intervals.icu. Delivery to MyWhoosh depends on the connected services and must be checked separately.

Google access with a specific purpose

The tool requests permission to manage files it creates or files you explicitly authorize. It creates a private training folder and notebook in your Drive. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

The CLI runs on the computer or cloud environment you choose. This informational website does not run the CLI or connect to your accounts.

Current availability

The project is currently in private development. It is not yet a public hosted service or a generally available download.
